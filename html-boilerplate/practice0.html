My day at UNILAK Campus

My day begins like any other student's day, I wake and try to set my clothes that I would wear. I later then make hot water for my bathing and hygene purpose and put on my clothes when I get through with it all.

Before entering the Campus premises

The first and most important part of the day is the ID verification being done at the gate by the securities. Without being verify you would not be allowed to enter the campus, so it is advice to make sure you always have your student ID on you.

The second most important part of the day

This would be the class attendance, before and after classes, as this attendance either qualify you to take your test and exams or restrict you from taking them. Which is why it is advice to keep your ID card on you when attending classes of coming on campus.
